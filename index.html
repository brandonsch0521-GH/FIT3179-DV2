How Has Participation in Queensland's Neighbourhood Centres Changed Over Time?

Weekly attendance, programmed activity participation, and service referrals from 2019 to 2025.

Weekly centre attendance increased from 35,245 in 2019 to 53,230 in 2025, highlighting the growing reach of Queensland's neighbourhood centres.

How Does Neighbourhood Centre Availability Vary Across Australian States?

Funded neighbourhood centres per 100,000 residents across five Australian states in 2025. Darker shades indicate higher availability.

Tasmania recorded the highest rate at 6.09 funded centres per 100,000 people, followed by Victoria at 5.73. Queensland had the lowest reported rate at 2.29. Grey states indicate unavailable data, not zero funded centres.
