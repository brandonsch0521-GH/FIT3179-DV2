THE TAKEAWAYS

What the data
tells us.

01 / PLACES

Centres connect communities geographically.

The 2019 Queensland location records cluster along the east coast. This says where centres are recorded—not how easily each community can reach them.

02 / PARTICIPATION

More people are engaging with centres.

Reported Queensland weekly centre attendance grew by about 51% between 2019 and 2025.

03 / SUPPORT

Practical support is a central part of the story.

In Queensland, the proportion of centres offering food relief rose from 68% to 87% between 2019 and 2025.

METHOD & SOURCES

Read the data responsibly.

Maps and comparisons are intended to highlight patterns, not to imply that all centres, states or years were measured in the same way.

Data Sources

01 / Queensland Sector Impact Report (2025) ↗ 02 / Victoria Annual Sector Survey (2024–25) ↗ 03 / Queensland Government Open Data (2019) ↗

Important distinctions

Queensland centre locations are from 2019. State-level funding rates cover five reported states only. Density surfaces are smoothed, relative estimates; grey or absent states should not be read as zero.
