Weekly attendance, programmed activity participation, and service referrals from 2019 to 2025.

How Has Emergency Relief Provision Changed in Queensland?

Percentage of Queensland neighbourhood centres providing food relief and non-food material relief between 2019 and 2025.

Food relief provision increased from 68% of centres in 2019 to 87% in 2025, while non-food material relief increased from 69% to 83%. This highlights the growing proportion of centres offering emergency assistance.

Which Digital Inclusion Services Are Most Widely Offered in Queensland?

Percentage of Queensland neighbourhood centres offering different digital inclusion opportunities in 2025.

Printing, copying and scanning services are the most widely available at 92%, followed by Wi-Fi at 82%. Advanced digital skills programs are offered by only 16% of centres, suggesting a gap between basic digital access and advanced digital learning opportunities.

How Does Weekly Neighbourhood Centre Participation Differ Across Queensland Communities?

Median weekly visitors per neighbourhood centre across four community types in Queensland, 2025.

Metropolitan neighbourhood centres recorded the highest median weekly visitation at 320 visitors, compared with 145 among centres in communities with fewer than 3,000 residents. These figures describe typical visitation per centre, not total visitation across each community type.

How Has Volunteering Changed in Victoria's Neighbourhood Houses?

Comparison of total volunteers and weekly volunteer hours between 2024 and 2025. Values are indexed to 2024 = 100 to allow percentage growth comparisons.

Victoria's neighbourhood houses experienced an 11.6% increase in total volunteers and a 9.0% increase in weekly volunteer hours between 2024 and 2025, highlighting increased volunteer contributions to community services.
